Describe & Draw

SEL / Counseling

Describe it. Listen. Draw it.

A quick partner drawing game that builds communication and active listening. One person describes a picture while the other draws from their words.

How to Play

Before you begin: Choose a grade band and round length, then select a built-in picture or upload your own.

01

Look

Speaker · 5 seconds
Study the picture before it hides. Notice the main shapes, sizes, positions, and details you will need to describe.

02

Describe

Speaker · explain with words
Guide your partner without showing the picture. Give clear clues about shapes, size, position, and details; the artist can ask questions when something is unclear.

03

Draw

Artist · listen and sketch
Draw from the speaker's directions without seeing the target. When the round ends, compare the pictures and discuss what helped you listen, explain, and work together.

The similarity score is a simple classroom estimate. The main goal is to practise clear communication, active listening, patience, and teamwork.
